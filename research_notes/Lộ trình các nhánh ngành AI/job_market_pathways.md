## 1. Demand trends 2025–2026: which roles are growing or shrinking?

### Takeaway
Demand for AI-specific engineering (AI Engineer, ML/AI researchers) and data infrastructure (big data, data engineering) is growing strongly worldwide. The clearest counter-trend is fewer **entry-level jobs in AI-exposed occupations**: US employment of 22–25-year-olds in highly AI-exposed jobs (software development among them) is ~16–20% below trend. The market is not shrinking overall. It is shifting upward in seniority and toward people who can build with and evaluate AI.

### Cited Findings
**Macro outlook (WEF Future of Jobs 2025, published Jan 2025) [Primary]**
- The fastest-growing jobs to 2030 include **big data specialists, fintech engineers and AI & machine learning specialists** (the top 3), followed by software & applications developers (#4) and security management specialists (#5). — [WEF story](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-the-fastest-growing-and-declining-jobs/); [WEF report PDF](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Future_of_Jobs_Report_2025.pdf)
- Job disruption is expected to affect 22% of jobs by 2030: 170M roles created and 92M displaced, for a net +78M. AI and information processing alone are projected to create 11M jobs and displace 9M. — [WEF story](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-the-fastest-growing-and-declining-jobs/); [WEF press release](https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-78-million-new-job-opportunities-by-2030-but-urgent-upskilling-needed-to-prepare-workforces/)
- "AI and big data" is the **fastest-growing skill**, followed by networks & cybersecurity and technological literacy. Analytical thinking is still the top core skill, rated essential by ~7 in 10 employers. 86% of employers expect AI and information processing to transform their business. — [WEF skills outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/3-skills-outlook/); [WEF jobs-of-the-future story](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-jobs-of-the-future-and-the-skills-you-need-to-get-them/)

**Entry-level squeeze ("Canaries in the Coal Mine", Stanford Digital Economy Lab) [Primary research, via snippets]**

- The **Aug 2026 update** finds no widespread displacement, but the gap for young workers has **widened to ~19%**. Declines are concentrated where AI *substitutes* for tasks. Where AI *complements* work, employment is flat or rising, especially for experienced workers. — [Stanford DEL news, Aug 2026](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/); [Aug 2026 PDF](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/08/Canaries_August2026.pdf)
- An Oct 2025 presentation of the work notes that software developers aged 22–25 saw a ~20% employment decline since late 2022. — [AAAS/Chandar slides](https://www.aaas.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/BharatChandar_CSGWest_AAASEPI_ShapingAIFutureInYourState_Oct2025.pdf)

**Data teams (dbt Labs State of Analytics Engineering 2025, n=459, fielded Oct–Dec 2024) [Vendor survey]**
- 40% of data teams reported headcount growth. 30% reported budget growth, vs 9% the year before, with AI named as the catalyst because AI needs high-quality data. 70% of analytics professionals use AI to help write code. — [dbt Labs report](https://www.getdbt.com/resources/state-of-analytics-engineering-2025); [dbt summary](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/state-of-analytics-engineering-2025-summary); [BigDATAwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/2025/04/18/dbt-labs-report-reveals-how-ai-is-boosting-data-budgets-and-team-growth/)

### Inferences
- **Growing:** AI Engineer (LLM/RAG/agents), ML/AI researcher, data engineering/"big data", and MLOps/AI infrastructure (by extension: agentic and infra skill growth, plus the need for high-quality data for AI). **Steady to growing but changing:** Data Scientist (BLS +34%, but the job is shifting toward ML and experimentation). **Most pressured at entry level:** generic junior coding and routine reporting/BI analyst work, which are the most AI-substitutable task bundles. No source isolates "Data Analyst postings" specifically. This is an inference from the Canaries substitution findings and the Vietnam reports below.
- The "AI Engineer" title is new and fuzzy. Part of its measured growth is relabeling of software/ML engineer roles, so growth rates overstate truly net-new jobs.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2025–2026 time series isolating postings for **Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer or MLOps Engineer** separately (global or Vietnam).
- WEF 2025 ranks job *categories*, not the exact titles listed in the brief. "Data Analyst/Scientist" appears in WEF's growth lists in past editions, but I did not verify its 2025 rank.
- The LinkedIn "+143%" and "56% wage premium" figures are unverified.

## 2. Vietnam: demand, salaries (VND/USD), policy and employers

### Takeaway
In Vietnam, AI and data roles are among the best-paid and hardest-to-fill IT jobs. AI engineers typically earn ~US$1,100–2,100/month (≈29–54M VND). Data engineers earn ~41M VND median, rising to ~57M VND at 3–4 years. Overall IT postings fell and the fresher market is tight ("thiếu người giỏi, dư người code cơ bản": a shortage of strong people and a surplus of basic coders). The state is pushing hard on AI: the AI Law is in force from 1 Mar 2026, and the national AI strategy (Aug 2026) targets 50,000 AI-applied workers and 10,000 advanced AI specialists by 2030.

### Inferences
- A realistic Vietnam salary ladder (monthly, gross, 2025–2026, pooled from the sources above):
  - Fresher/junior DA or AI: ~11–25M VND (US$420–960).
  - Mid-level DS/DE/AI (2–4 yrs): ~30–57M VND (US$1,150–2,200).
  - Senior/lead: 50–90M+ VND (US$1,900–3,500+).

## 3. Global salary references (with caveats)

### Takeaway
US AI/ML engineering pay is far above Vietnam's: ML Engineer median total compensation is ~US$250–280k on Levels.fyi, vs ~US$112k BLS median for data scientists. The figures differ widely by source because each measures something different (self-reported big-tech total comp vs whole-economy base wage).

### Cited Findings
- **Levels.fyi (live pages, retrieved Sep 2026; US; total comp incl. stock):** Machine Learning Engineer median **~$280k**. ML/AI-focused SWE median ~$250k. The "AI Engineer" title median is **~$154k** (lower because it includes many non-big-tech firms). By company, MLE median is ~$479k at Meta, ~$366k Apple, ~$290k Google, ~$280k Amazon and ~$244k Nvidia. — [Levels.fyi MLE](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/title/machine-learning-engineer); [Levels.fyi ML/AI focus](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/focus/ml-ai); [Levels.fyi AI Engineer](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/title/ai-engineer); [Levels.fyi 2025 report](https://levels.fyi/2025)
- **BLS (whole US economy, base wage):** Data scientists' median was $112,590 (May 2024). The bottom 10% earned <$63,650 and the top 10% >$194,410. — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm?id=1940)
- **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 (US, via secondary analysis):** data scientist average ~$145k, down ~8.8% from $159k in 2024. AI/ML engineer ~$189.5k. The median rose 5–29% for 20 roles. [Secondary; not verified on SO site because it was blocked] — [techrecruiting.io analysis](https://techrecruiting.io/en/stack-overflow-developer-survey-2025-usa/); [SO survey: Work](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/work)
- **Lightcast:** the median advertised salary for AI jobs reportedly rose from $84.4k (2022) to $181.1k (2025). This figure comes from a search snippet summary and is likely a specific subset of postings, so treat it cautiously. — [Lightcast GenAI 2025](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/the-generative-ai-job-market-2025-data-insights)

### Inferences
- Caveats for a Vietnamese learner:
  - Levels.fyi is dominated by big-tech self-reports and includes equity, so it is an upper bound.
  - BLS is base pay across all employers.
  - US figures are not directly transferable. Vietnam AI Engineer ≈ US$13–25k/yr vs US ≈ US$150–280k.
  - The realistic arbitrage routes are remote/offshore roles or Singapore, which leads the world in AI posting share per the AI Index.
- The drop in data-scientist pay alongside rising AI/ML-engineer pay (SO 2025) matches the market shift from "analysis/modeling" to "shipping AI systems".

## 4. Interview loops per role and what credentials actually matter

### Takeaway
Loops differ by branch, but all of them test the job itself:
- Analysts and data scientists: SQL + metrics/product sense + statistics/A-B testing.
- Data engineers: SQL + data modeling + pipeline design.
- ML engineers: coding + ML theory + ML system design.
- AI engineers: LLM/RAG system design + **evals** + cost and latency trade-offs.

Portfolios of real end-to-end projects count for more than certifications. Cloud certs mainly get a CV past keyword filters.

### Cited Findings
- **Data Scientist/Analyst (product DS):** loops at top companies run 4–6 rounds (coding, statistics/ML, product sense/case, system design for seniors, behavioral). SQL is embedded in business questions: compute a metric, then interpret it or decide whether to launch. Product-sense rounds cover metric definition, feature evaluation and experiment analysis. Phone-screen cases take 10–15 min and onsite cases 30–45 min. [Interview-prep vendors] — [Interview Query: DS case study](https://www.interviewquery.com/p/data-science-case-study-interview-questions); [DataLemur: Meta DS guide](https://datalemur.com/blog/meta-data-scientist-interview-guide); [Hacking the Case Interview](https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/pages/data-science-case-interview)
- **AI Engineer (2026):** loops focus on system design over a model you do not control, judgment about retrieval and **evaluation**, building and debugging the integration by hand, and production sense (cost, latency, failure modes). Five clusters cover most loops: LLM/transformer basics, RAG architecture, agentic systems, prompting + evals, and LLM product system design. A typical prompt is "design an enterprise knowledge-base agent", with trade-offs between embedding latency, retrieval accuracy and token cost. RAG evals are framed as faithfulness, answer relevance and context relevance, often with LLM-as-judge. [Vendor/blog tier] — [KORE1 AI Engineer interview questions 2026](https://www.kore1.com/ai-engineer-interview-questions-2026/); [PracHub GenAI system design 2026](https://prachub.com/resources/genai-llm-system-design-interview-guide-2026); [Chirag Hasija, 2026](https://chiraghasija.cc/posts/how-to-interview-ai-engineer-role-2026/)
- In Vietnam, companies now test "collaboration with AI" as well as algorithms. — [VnExpress, Jun 2026](https://vnexpress.net/thi-truong-it-viet-thieu-nguoi-gioi-du-nguoi-code-co-ban-5059102.html)
- **Certifications:**
  - AWS certs appear in ~51,000 job listings (out of 12M+ tech postings) and act as keywords for automated CV screening.
  - GCP data/ML certs are said to carry weight at BigQuery/Vertex shops.
  - A candidate with one relevant cert (e.g., AWS Data Engineer Associate, DEA-C01) plus 2 production-like pipeline projects is presented as a clearer hire than one with six certs and no coherent story.
  - Claimed pay premiums ($15–30k for AWS-certified engineers) come from vendor blogs and are unverified.

  — [Dataquest: data engineering certs 2026](https://www.dataquest.io/blog/best-data-engineering-certifications/); [Digital Cloud Training](https://digitalcloud.training/which-cloud-certifications-will-get-you-hired-in-2026/); [Towards Data Engineering: 1,000 postings analysis](https://medium.com/towards-data-engineering/i-analyzed-1-000-data-engineering-job-postings-heres-which-certifications-actually-matter-in-2026-544fb1594d79)
- **Portfolios:** hiring managers want end-to-end data engineering projects (ingestion → transform → load → monitoring/data-quality checks) built with Airflow/Spark/Snowflake-type tools. One deep, documented project beats many shallow repos. Portfolios matter most for career switchers, bootcamp graduates and juniors. [Blog tier] — [Data Engineer Academy checklist 2026](https://dataengineeracademy.com/blog/data-engineer-portfolio-review-checklist-2026-what-hiring-managers-actually-score/); [DataExpert.io](https://www.dataexpert.io/blog/data-engineering-portfolio-projects-get-hired)
- One widely repeated claim, "73% of hiring managers value a portfolio over a résumé (SO 2024)", **could not be verified**. The Stack Overflow survey does not survey hiring managers that way, so treat it as likely spurious. — [DataExpert.io (where it appears)](https://www.dataexpert.io/blog/data-engineering-portfolio-projects-get-hired)

## 5. Career pathways, transitions and time-to-competence

### Takeaway
All branches share a common foundation: Python, SQL, statistics, data wrangling, Git and communication. From there they split into three families:
- **Analytics:** DA → Analytics Engineer / DS.
- **Engineering/infra:** DE → MLE/MLOps.
- **AI product:** SWE → AI Engineer.

Research/Applied Scientist usually requires graduate research. The most common, fastest transitions use an existing adjacent skill: SWE → AI Engineer, DE → MLE/MLOps, DA → Analytics Engineer/DS. Credible time-to-competence estimates are scarce, and the ones that exist come from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- LinkedIn's most common AI Engineer skills (LangChain, RAG, PyTorch) combine software engineering and ML, which explains why SWEs are the main feeder into AI Engineer roles. — [Dice on LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise 2026](https://www.dice.com/career-advice/ai-related-jobs-top-linkedins-fastest-growing-roles-list-for-2026)
- Vendor and blog estimates:
  - SWE → AI Engineer ≈ 3–6 months. "90-day plan" readers reportedly reach first-round interviews in 3–4 months.
  - Data Analyst → AI Engineer ≈ 9–12 months, because the missing pieces are SWE discipline, ML depth and deployment.
  - Beginner → data scientist training ≈ 6–12 months, or 1–2 months if already strong in programming and stats.

  These are **not rigorous estimates**. — [jobsbyculture.com](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/transitioning-to-ai-roles-2026); [ClassForJobs](https://classforjobs.com/blog-details/data-analyst-to-ai-engineer-your-2026-transition-plan/208); [365 Data Science](https://365datascience.com/career-advice/transition-into-data-science-career/); [ODSC, Jul 2026](https://odsc.medium.com/from-analyst-to-ai-engineer-how-to-make-the-career-leap-f8f175760ed5)
- In dbt's 2025 survey, 48% of individual contributors were analytics engineers, 36% data engineers and 16% data analysts. This shows analytics engineering as an established title that bridges DA and DE. — [dbt Labs 2025 summary](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/state-of-analytics-engineering-2025-summary)
- The Stanford Canaries data show experienced workers in AI-exposed jobs holding steady while 22–25-year-olds decline. This supports entering via adjacent roles and building experience, rather than targeting senior-only titles straight out of school. — [Stanford DEL Aug 2026](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/)
- Vietnam: firms prefer experienced hires, and "entry-level" posts often ask for 2–5 years. — [VnExpress, Jun 2026](https://vnexpress.net/thi-truong-it-viet-thieu-nguoi-gioi-du-nguoi-code-co-ban-5059102.html)

### Inferences — role-connection map (synthesis)
- **Shared core (all 8 roles):** Python, SQL, statistics/probability, data cleaning, Git, cloud basics, communication with stakeholders.
- **Common transitions (lowest to highest friction):**
  - DA → Analytics Engineer: add dbt, data modeling and testing.
  - DA → Data Scientist: add ML, experimentation and deeper stats.
  - SWE → Data Engineer or AI Engineer: add SQL/data modeling, or LLM APIs, RAG and evals.
  - DE → MLOps/ML Engineer: add ML fundamentals, feature stores and model serving.
  - DS → ML Engineer: add software engineering, system design and deployment.
  - MLE → Applied Scientist: add research depth, usually a Master's/PhD or publications.
  - Anyone → Research Scientist is the hardest and usually requires a PhD.
- **Realistic entry doors for a Vietnamese learner in 2026:**
  1. Data Analyst / BI at banks, e-commerce or fintech. There are many openings, but they are the most AI-exposed, so pair the role with domain knowledge and analytics-engineering skills.
  2. Junior Data Engineer, a high-growth door in Vietnam (+38% postings).
  3. SWE → AI Engineer, the fastest pay growth but a high bar.

### Gaps
- I found no credible longitudinal data (e.g., LinkedIn Economic Graph transition matrices) on actual transition rates between these roles, globally or in Vietnam. There is no rigorous time-to-competence study.

## 6. How AI assistants change productivity and hiring; skills now emphasized

### Takeaway
Evidence on productivity is mixed. Controlled studies show large gains on tasks inside AI's "frontier" (consultants: +12% tasks, 25% faster, higher quality), but a 19% *slowdown* for experienced developers on complex, familiar codebases. Developer trust in AI output is low. Employers therefore increasingly reward **judgment, evaluation/verification, problem framing, domain knowledge and communication** over rote coding, and this raises the bar for juniors.

### Cited Findings
- **METR RCT (Jul 2025):** 16 experienced open-source developers worked on 246 real tasks in mature repositories (22k+ stars, 1M+ LOC). With AI tools (mainly Cursor Pro with Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet) they were **19% slower**, although they had forecast a 24% speed-up and afterwards believed they had been 20% faster. — [METR blog](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/); [arXiv 2507.09089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089)
- METR changed its experiment design in Feb 2026. The Jul 2025 result is specific to early-2025 tools. — [METR Feb 2026 update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/)
- **Harvard/BCG "Jagged Technological Frontier" (Sep 2023):** with 758 BCG consultants using GPT-4, those with AI completed **12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, at >40% higher quality**. On a task outside the frontier they were **19 percentage points less likely** to be correct. The search snippet said "40 percent of the trial group" produced higher quality; the paper reports >40% higher quality, so this is a snippet wording issue. — [HBS AI Institute](https://aiinstitute.hbs.edu/navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier/); [Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2023/10/13/jagged-edge-ai-bcg/)
- **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025:**
  - 84% use or plan to use AI tools, up from 76% in 2024.
  - Positive sentiment fell from 70%+ to 60%.
  - 46% distrust AI accuracy vs 33% who trust it, and only 3% "highly trust" it.
  - 45% say debugging AI-generated code is time-consuming.

  — [Stack Overflow press release](https://stackoverflow.co/company/press/archive/stack-overflow-2025-developer-survey/); [SO survey AI section](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai); [SO blog](https://stackoverflow.blog/2025/12/29/developers-remain-willing-but-reluctant-to-use-ai-the-2025-developer-survey-results-are-here/)
- **Anthropic Economic Index:** usage splits roughly 57% augmentation and 43% automation. Use is concentrated in mid-to-high-wage roles such as programmers and **data scientists**. Coding tasks are migrating from augmentative chat use toward more automated API workflows. — [Anthropic Economic Index intro](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-anthropic-economic-index); [Jan 2026 report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report); [Mar 2026 report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report)
- **dbt 2025:** 70% of analytics professionals use AI for code and 50% for documentation. Data quality is the #1 challenge, and AI is raising investment in data quality. — [dbt Labs](https://www.getdbt.com/blog/state-of-analytics-engineering-2025-summary)
- **Skills employers emphasize:**
  - WEF 2025: analytical thinking is the top core skill (~70% of employers); AI and big data is the fastest-growing skill, followed by resilience/flexibility and leadership/social influence. — [WEF skills outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/3-skills-outlook/)
  - In AI Engineer loops, candidates who get offers have built something hands-on, explain trade-offs plainly and treat evals as a first-class discipline. — [KORE1](https://www.kore1.com/ai-engineer-interview-questions-2026/)
  - Vietnam: AI "raises hiring standards" rather than "stealing jobs". Employers want problem-solving, business understanding, critical thinking (not over-reliance on AI) and soft skills. — [VnExpress, Jun 2026](https://vnexpress.net/thi-truong-it-viet-thieu-nguoi-gioi-du-nguoi-code-co-ban-5059102.html); [VnExpress: Nỗi lo tụt hậu của lập trình viên khi dùng AI](https://vnexpress.net/noi-lo-tut-hau-cua-lap-trinh-vien-khi-dung-ai-5089747.html)
- **Employment effect:** declines concentrate where AI substitutes for tasks, and entry-level roles are hit first. Where AI complements work, employment is flat or rising. — [Stanford DEL Aug 2026](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/)

### Inferences
- In hiring, AI productivity gains translate into (a) fewer pure-execution junior roles, and (b) a premium on people who can *specify, verify and evaluate* AI output. That means knowing when the model is outside its "frontier", which matches the METR and BCG results.
- For learners, the most defensible skills are:
  - statistics and experiment design;
  - data modeling and data quality;
  - evaluation of ML/LLM systems;
  - domain knowledge in finance, e-commerce or telco, Vietnam's big employers;
  - clear written communication of decisions.
- Using AI tools fluently is now expected, and interviews in Vietnam already test it.

### Gaps
- There is no RCT specifically on data analysts or data scientists (SQL/analysis work) with 2025–2026 tools. Evidence is extrapolated from developer and consultant studies.
- There is no Vietnam-specific quantitative study on AI's effect on junior hiring in data roles (only news and survey claims).
